    BATTLE

    How to play

    One opponent. Five seconds. Every tap counts.

    1. Get close

      Walk up to another player, then click or tap their avatar. You need to be nearby to challenge them.

    2. Send a challenge

      Choose Fight. Your opponent can Accept or Decline.

    3. Tap for power

      Wait for the 3-second countdown. Then tap POWER! as fast as you can for 5 seconds. Each tap adds one point.

    4. Check the result

      More taps wins. Equal scores mean a draw. Wait 5 seconds before your next battle.
